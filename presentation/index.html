🛠️ 4-Stage Preparation Protocol
  • ▶
    Standardization: 24 kHz Mono WAV conversion, RMS energy normalization, clipping diagnostics.
  • ▶
    VAD Speech Slicing: Energy-based Voice Activity Detection into tight 2s–15s utterance windows.
  • ▶
    Whisper large-v3 ASR: Multilingual speech-to-text with beam search and interactive CLI review.
  • ▶
    Kaldi & Parquet Export: Generates wav.scp, text, utt2spk, and PyArrow tables.

ItsMe Voice AI Platform Slide 4 of 8
The golden rule in voice AI is: quality data beats model scale. ItsMe automates the heavy lifting of audio engineering. It cleans the recordings, normalizes everything to 24kHz, trims silence, and automatically transcribes speech using Whisper large-v3. We also provide a human-in-the-loop CLI tool for swift transcript corrections before producing deterministic 90/5/5 train-validation-test splits.
Model Strategy CosyVoice 3 0.5B

Foundation Model & Fine-Tuning

Flow matching architecture conditioned on speaker and discrete speech tokens

05 / 08
🧩 S3Tokenizer
Converts continuous audio into discrete, rich speech tokens (utt2speech_token.pt), capturing prosodic inflection and rhythm.
🧬 CAMPPlus Embeddings
Extracts high-dimension biometric speaker representations (spk2embedding.pt), locking in your personal vocal timbre.
🌊 Flow Matching 0.5B
Diffusion-based flow matching model fine-tuned with gradient accumulation, producing artifact-free 24kHz mel-spectrograms.
💻 Hardware Flexibility & Optimization

Apple Silicon: Native MPS acceleration for Mac Mini M4 / M-Series chips.
CPU Fallback: Full CPU compatibility for zero-dependency local testing.
